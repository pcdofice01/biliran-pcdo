About the Office

Provincial Cooperatives and Development Office

Serving cooperative organizations and communities throughout the Province of Biliran.

Who We Are

The Provincial Cooperatives and Development Office (PCDO) is a provincial government office dedicated to strengthening the cooperative sector in Biliran. The office works with cooperatives, municipalities, national government agencies, partner institutions, and community stakeholders to promote sustainable and inclusive cooperative development.

Our Vision

A progressive and empowered cooperative sector contributing to inclusive and sustainable development in Biliran.

Our Mission

To provide responsive technical assistance, capacity building, coordination, monitoring, and development support to cooperatives.

Our Core Values

We promote professionalism, transparency, accountability, service, participation, cooperation, innovation, and community empowerment.

Integrity

Responsible and ethical public service.

Service

People-centered and responsive assistance.

Cooperation

Working together toward shared goals.

Innovation

Practical solutions for evolving cooperative needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

Common questions about cooperative development assistance.

What services does the PCDO provide?

The office provides cooperative development assistance, technical assistance, capability building, coordination, monitoring, and other development support.

Who can request assistance?

Cooperatives, cooperative officers and members, prospective cooperative organizers, partner institutions, and other stakeholders may coordinate with the office regarding appropriate services.

Where is the PCDO located?

The Provincial Cooperatives and Development Office is located at the Provincial Capitol, Naval, Biliran.

How can we contact the office?

Use the contact details below or visit the office during official government working hours.
